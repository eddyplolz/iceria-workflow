# Answer a "can I do this?" question

1. **Find the rule or the fact.** Look in this order: `style/charter-extract.md` and
   `style/ai-use-on-iceria.md` for conduct and ownership; `canon/world-standards.md`,
   `canon/geography.json` and `canon/history-of-iceria.md` for the world; `canon/nations/` for
   other nations; `style/manual-of-style.md` for how a page is written.
2. **Answer in one or two sentences, with the citation**: the file and the section or line, for
   example "Charter 3.8". Quote the deciding words when they are short.
3. **Say what it needs.** When the answer is "yes, with someone's agreement", name whose: the other
   nation's creator, or a steward for a new nation or a world-wide change.
4. **When nothing in the kit covers it, say so.** A missing page means "not written yet" (Charter
   1.4), so the idea is open: tell the member it is theirs to decide for their own nation, and that
   the live wiki or a steward can confirm. Never invent a rule.
5. **Leave the choice to the member.** Lay out what the sources say and, where it helps, 2 or 3
   ways to do it that fit them. Do not pick for them.

Done when the member has an answer with its source, or knows the kit has none and who to ask.
