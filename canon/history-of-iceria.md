# History of Iceria

'''The history of Iceria''' is the shared past of the planet Iceria, the backdrop every nation on
this wiki builds its own story on. It runs on the same broad lines as Earth's. People first spread
out of the continent of Mesaria, the first cities rose in four river lands, and merchants riding
the monsoon linked three continents for two thousand years before the age of sail. From about
1450, ships from the Old World reached three frontier continents, bringing disease, conquest and
colonies, and between about 1780 and 1830 most of Septentria and Meridia won independence. A great
war ended in 1945, the Concord of Nations was founded that same year, and by 2000, the first year
of play, the world is divided among modern states, almost all of them members of the
Concord.<ref name="ws-history" />

This page sets only the broad strokes. It names no empire, kingdom or state except Leria, the
classical republic and empire that [[Alexandria]] wrote and descends from, Alexandria itself, and
those the founding nations ([[Charter]] 3.11) wrote, such as [[Mantua]]; all others belong to the
players who write them. A new nation may be centuries old or newly independent, native, settler
or mixed, as long as its story fits the outline here.

== The shape of the world ==
[[File:Iceria-physical.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The land of Iceria by height, with the Dorsal Range at the middle of Mesaria]]
[[File:Iceria-holy-lands-thalassine-crossing.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The Thalassine crossing from Eostria to Mesaria, about 1,200 kilometers at the closest points]]

Iceria has six continents. Three of them form the Old World: Eostria in the north, Mesaria in the
middle and Solandra in the south-east. Eostria lies about 1,200 kilometers from each of the other
two at the closest points, near enough for early boats, while Mesaria and Solandra lie some 7,300
kilometers apart, on opposite sides of the Ecliptic Ocean. The other three, the frontier
continents, lie farther out: Septentria across the Thalassine Ocean, Meridia to the south-west,
and Selenara to the south of Solandra.<ref name="cz" /> A few early voyages reached them, but they
were drawn into lasting contact with the Old World only in the age of sail.

The oceans were highways more than barriers. The Thalassine, the sea at the middle of the world,
joins Eostria's west coast to Mesaria's north and, much farther west, to Septentria. The Ecliptic
Ocean, under the sun's path, links the trade coasts of Mesaria's east, Eostria's south and
Solandra. Its monsoon winds reverse with the seasons, which let sailors cross it and come home in
the same year.<ref name="routes" />

== First peoples ==

Humankind began in Mesaria, in the high country around the Dorsal Range.<ref name="ws-history" /> From there people spread
north into Eostria and east into Solandra, and in very early times across the sea to Selenara.
During the last ice age, when the seas stood lower, hunters crossed the narrow northern strait from
Eostria's far north-east into Septentria. Their descendants spread south through the whole
continent and crossed the narrow sea to Meridia. Septentria, Meridia and Selenara were then
settled for many thousands of years before the Old World's ships arrived.

== The river cradles ==
[[File:Iceria-river-cradles.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The four river cradles of the Old World and the two frontier highlands where farming began on its own]]
[[File:Iceria-farmland.png|thumb|upright=1.6|Farmland on Iceria, graded by how well the land suits crops]]

Farming and the first cities began, about five thousand years before 2000, in four river lands:

* The Origo valley. The Origo rises in the cool highlands near the Dorsal Range and runs north through the Numidic desert to the Thalassine.<ref name="geo" /> Its yearly flood made a strip of rich farmland in the desert, and one of the world's oldest kingdoms grew along it.
* The lower Aurorea. Eostria's great river reaches the Ecliptic Ocean where the Ausonic east coast meets the Sarmatic plains and the Scythic steppe. Its delta saw some of the first writing, and its cities traded both up the river and along the coast.
* Solandra's twin rivers. Two large rivers of Solandra's dry north and west fed early cities and a civilization that later spread across the whole continent.<ref name="cz" />
* The warm Seric south. A small, warm corner at the southern end of the Seric zone, in Eostria's far east beyond 120° E, fed the first Seric states, from which Seric culture spread north into a much colder land.

Farming also began on its own in the frontier continents, in the Altissiman highlands of Septentria
and the Orean highlands of Meridia, where great native civilizations rose long before any contact
with the Old World.

== Empires and trade seas ==

Over the next three thousand years empires rose and fell across the Old World. Sea empires of the
Ausonic coasts ruled the Thalassine shores and carried their languages, laws and roads far inland.
From the Scythic steppe, horse peoples built vast empires that reached at times from Seric lands
to the Ausonic coast. Seric dynasties ruled the east and kept a written record unbroken for
millennia. Kingdoms of the Occasic savanna grew rich on gold and the trade across the Numidic
desert.

[[File:Iceria-holy-lands-lerian-empire.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The Lerian Empire at its greatest extent]]
About 2,480 years before 2000 the towns of the lower Lère valley joined in the Lerian Republic,
with its capital at Leria, where the Corcovado meets the Lère. About 2,040 years before 2000 the
Republic became an empire. At its greatest extent the Lerian Empire ruled the Ausonic and Hesperic
lands, most of the Numidic lands across the sea and parts of the Thulean and Sarmatic lands to the
north and east. Its language, Lerian, became the classical language of the Ausonic lands. From about 450 the empire gave up its provinces one by one,
but its heartland on the Lère was never conquered, and its seats became the kingdoms from which
[[Alexandria]] descends.<ref name="alex" /> Lerian is the classical root of Alexandrian and
Martino,<ref name="alex" /> and Mantuan, the island tongue of [[Mantua]], is of Lerian
root.<ref name="mantua" /> Leria itself is now Geneva, the see of the Pontiff of Leria, head of
the Lerian Soterian Church.<ref name="alex-lead" /> Every nation whose land lies inside the
Lerian Empire's greatest extent was once ruled by Leria, and chooses its own part in that rule,
from loyal province to rebel frontier.<ref name="ws-history" />

The Ecliptic Ocean became the busiest sea of the Old World. Erythraean, Numidic, Solandric and
Nesiotic merchants carried spices, cloth, gold and ideas between three continents, and their
trading towns mixed languages and faiths. Nesiotic sailors, the boldest of them, crossed to
Selenara's north coast in the first millennium and settled among its Austronesian-anchored peoples,
where their descendants still live.<ref name="cz" /> Astronomers of the Ecliptic coasts fixed the epoch from which
most of the world now counts its years. The merchants carried this common reckoning along their
routes; many peoples kept their own calendars beside it, and the Concord of Nations adopted it for
international use in 1945. The year 2000 is counted from it.<ref name="ws-history" /> In the far north, Thulean seafarers reached Septentria's icy
north-east around the year 1000 and kept a few small stations there.

== Faiths ==
[[File:Iceria-holy-lands-faith-origins.png|thumb|upright=1.6|Where the Diathecan, Soterian and Deditan faiths were born]]
[[File:Iceria-holy-lands-deditan-spread.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The Deditan faith's spread across Mesaria's north, the Ecliptic trade coasts and the Scythic steppe]]

The great faiths of the Old World grew from the cradles and traveled the trade routes. The
Soterian faith, a faith of one god born on the Ausonic east coast, spread west across the Ausonic
and Hesperic lands, north to the Thulean and Sarmatic peoples, and later with the settlers to the
frontier continents. The older Diathecan faith, born on the same coast, lives on in communities
across the Old World. The Deditan faith, a second faith of one god, born in the Numidic desert,
spread across Mesaria's north, along the Ecliptic trade coasts and onto the Scythic steppe. In Solandra the Ritan faith of
eternal order and the younger Vigilan faith of the wheel took shape; the Vigilan faith spread to
the Chrysic lands of Solandra's south and east and to the Seric east, where it joined the older
Lararian rites of duty to family and ancestors. Native and folk traditions continue across all
six continents. The [[#Faith traditions|table at the end of this page]] gives each tradition's
meaning and Earth anchor.<ref name="ws-history" />

== The age of sail ==
[[File:Iceria-winds-sea-routes.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The sea winds in January and July and the routes on this page, each in the season it sails fastest]]

The first ships to reach Meridia came from Mesaria. In the fourteenth century Occasic sailors,
carried by the south-east trade winds from Mesaria's south-west cape, crossed about 2,000
kilometers of open sea and reached Meridia's coast. Their landings were few and founded no lasting
settlement. They left stories on both shores and a few traces on Meridia's coast, and the later
sailors who took the same route knew it from them.

From about 1450 the age of sail opened on two oceans. On the Thalassine, the Hesperic and Ausonic
kingdoms of Eostria's west coast and the Thulean north sent fleets west and south. On the Ecliptic,
the trading states of the Erythraean, Numidic, Solandric and Nesiotic coasts, which had sailed that
ocean for two thousand years, pushed their own ships and merchant colonies farther, sometimes as
rivals of the Thalassine powers and sometimes as partners. The Seric states, which had the ships to
join them, turned inward instead. The winds set the routes.<ref name="routes" /> Hesperic and Ausonic ships sailed down
Mesaria's north-west coast and caught the trade winds west across the Thalassine to Corallia, the
large tropical island off Septentria's southern arm, and on to the mainland. Others ran downwind from Mesaria's south-west
cape to Meridia, the old Occasic route, and Ecliptic merchants followed them to found trading
towns on Meridia's dry east coast. Thulean and Hesperic ships rode the westerlies of the
far south from Mesaria's southern cape to Selenara, where Solandran and Nesiotic settlers had
arrived first, and on to the cold far south of Meridia.

Contact between 1450 and 1550 was followed by conquest. Diseases from the Old World killed far
more native people than war did, and many regions lost much of their population within a
century.
Colonies spread through the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. On the plantation coasts of
Septentria and Meridia the colonists relied on forced labor, and a slave trade carried large numbers of
Mesarian people, chiefly from the Occasic coasts and their neighbors, across the Thalassine. Their descendants are a large part of those coasts'
populations today.

The result differed by continent, as the [[Culture zones of Iceria#The frontier continents|frontier table]] records.<ref name="cz" /> Septentria's east, with the
best harbor in the world and wide farmland,<ref name="geo" /> drew the largest settler wave. The highland peoples of
the Altissima and of Meridia's Orean highlands were conquered but not replaced, and they remain the
majority there. Selenara's north coast never saw a large Old World settler wave.

== Independence ==
[[File:Iceria-culture-zones.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Iceria]]

The settler colonies broke away first. Between about 1780 and 1830 most of Septentria and Meridia
won independence, some by war and some by agreement. Native nations made treaties with the new
states, kept their homelands, or stayed independent where no treaty was signed, as the [[Culture zones of Iceria#The frontier continents|frontier table]] records zone by zone;<ref name="cz" /> where they had been strongest, in the highlands and in Selenara's
north, they remained the majority.

Across the Old World, the industrial age of the nineteenth century brought railways, steamships
and factories, and the Old World's sea powers took new colonies, ports and concessions along the coasts of
Mesaria, Solandra and the Ecliptic islands. Some states modernized and kept their independence;
others resisted for decades. A second wave of independence followed in the twentieth century
and was largely over by 1970.

== The modern century ==

From 1938 to 1945 the [[Great War]] drew in much of the world. Nuclear weapons
were first built at its end. When it ended in 1945, the victors and many smaller states founded
the Concord of Nations the same year to keep the peace, and almost every independent state has
since joined it. After the war the nuclear powers handed their bombs to the new Concord and stopped
building more, under the [[International Nuclear Compact]]. The bomb was never used against a city.
The decades that followed brought a divided peace between rival powers, the last of the
independence waves, and rapid technical change, and by 2000 the divisions were easing.

In the year 2000 technology matches Earth's of the same year: jet travel, satellites, mobile
telephones and an early internet. No nation is proven to hold a nuclear weapon, though a few
unwritten countries are suspected of hidden programs.<ref name="ws-history" />

== Iceria in 2000 ==

Almost all of Iceria's land is lived on and governed. Most of it is not yet written
([[World Standards]] 12). A newcomer's nation takes its place in this history: it may be centuries old or
newly independent, native, settler or mixed. It writes its own empires, wars and heroes, as long
as they fit the broad strokes on this page.

A nation of the Old World may have been a colonizer, a colony, a protectorate, a trading partner
or never colonized at all. No nation may make another player's nation its colonizer, its enslaver
or its wartime enemy without that player's agreement. Where a written nation's history reaches land
that no player has written yet, such as a church's reach or a colonial power's colonies, the
player who writes a nation there chooses whether it shared that history. Leria's rule over its
greatest extent is the one exception: it is fixed for every nation inside that extent.<ref name="ws-history" />

== What this page fixes and what it leaves open ==
''Adopted 4 October 2026 as section 13 of the [[World Standards]]. A nation's own
history must fit it.''

The names this page uses are the world map's (continents, seas, rivers, ranges, islands), the
culture families' ([[Culture zones of Iceria]]) and, for Leria and the founding nations' states,
those of the nations that wrote them. It names no empire, kingdom or state except Leria, the
classical republic and empire that [[Alexandria]] wrote and descends from, Alexandria itself, and
those the founding nations wrote ([[Charter]] 3.11). All others belong to the nations that write
them ([[World Standards]] 6.4 and 12).

No player nation starts with nuclear weapons. The bombs of the Great War are held in Concord
custody, no nation is proven to hold one in 2000, and the few unwritten countries suspected of hidden
programs are not yet named. The stewards may open nuclear weapons to established nations by a
change to the [[World Standards]] (13.5).

== Faith traditions ==

The great faiths have in-world names, each anchored on an Earth faith in the way the culture
families are anchored on Earth cultures. The anchors are inspiration only: every nation writes its
own doctrines, schisms and practice, and the modern standing of every faith community is the
players' to write. [[Names of Iceria]] lists them beside the 31 culture families.
