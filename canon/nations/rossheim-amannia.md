## Lead

'''Rossheim-Amannia''', officially the '''Grand Duchy of Rossheim and Kingdom of Amannia''' (Saxon: ''Grossherzogtum Rossheim und Königreich Amannien''; Amannian: ''Storhertogadómrinn Hrossheim ok Konungríkit Ámannaland''), is a dual monarchy in western Eostria and eastern Hyperbora. A real union of two self-governing states under a single monarch, it was established in 1448 when the Duke of Rossheim was elected king of neighbouring Amannia. The two states are united in a custom union and share a common foreign policy, but otherwise maintain separate legal and constitutional identities; whereas the monarch's power is effectively unlimited within Rossheim, Amannia is a constitutional monarchy in which the king shares authority with an elected assembly, the Ámannathing.
