## Lead

'''Mantua''', officially the '''Principality of Mantua''', is an island country about 270 km off the south-west coast of [[Alexandria]], under Alexandrian protection. It covers 4,509 km², a flat island with a single crag, and had about 800,000 people in 2000.<ref name="proposal" /> Its capital, San Leopoldo en la Piedra, stands on the crag around the Basilica on the Rock (Basílica en la Piedra), the church cut into the rock above the shrine of St Leopoldo. The capital is a resort and a center of finance, and farm towns spread across the flat land inland.

The island grew up around the shrine of St Leopoldo, a holy man beatified in 1389. The Cavaletta family bought it in 1406, and in 1632 it passed to the [[House of Montillet]], which has reigned since then. Mantua has been an independent principality since 1705 and under Alexandrian protection since 1714: Alexandria defends it and represents it abroad, and it uses the Alexandrian écu.<ref name="proposal" /> It has been a constitutional monarchy since 1895. [[Fabiola, Princess of Mantua|Princess Fabiola]] has reigned since 1978, and her government is led by a Chief Minister and answers to an elected National Council of 30 members. Most Mantuans belong to the Lerian Soterian Church, and Mantuans speak three languages: their own Mantuan, Alexandrian and Martino. Since 1986 the former Alexandrian emperor [[Edgard I]] has lived on the island in exile.
