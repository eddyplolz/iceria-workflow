## Lead

'''Seltana''' is a country in central [[Mesaria]], on the upper Origo. It holds the headwaters of the Origo, which rises inside its borders at 0°, 0°, and a warm central basin ringed by cool highlands and walled by the western ridge and the crest of the Dorsal Range. Its area is 2,510,903 km², and it had about 30 million people in 2000. The capital and largest city is [[Amba Abeba]], a stone city cut into a flat-topped mountain on the central plateau.

Seltana has never been colonized. Its people can do what no other people can: when two of them meet each other's eyes they are linked for life, each aware of the other's feelings, and a death is felt by everyone linked to the dead. Its oldest institution, the [[Tarat]], uses these links to sense suffering early, prevent deaths and prepare the living for the deaths it cannot prevent. As of 2000 that care reaches every citizen: Seltana is orderly and closed, has little crime and a very low rate of unexplained deaths, and its people have almost no private life.

Seltana is a federation of self-governing passes and valleys called wardenships, under a ceremonial monarchy known as the Loom. Its political order rests on four institutions: the wardenships and their federal Council, the Tarat, the Loom and the Hall of Voices. The High Warden and the Council govern the state; the Tarat keeps the national Registry of Links and is the priesthood of [[Abebat]]; the Loom recognizes the other institutions and arbitrates between them; and the Hall of Voices represents citizens directly, including unlinked Seltanans, who cannot link. These institutions arose at different times and reached their present relationship through centuries of conflict and compromise.

Seltana lives mostly from its own farms, herds and weaving, with a GDP of about 45 billion IRU in 2000, and its currency is the Seltanan sel. Its main exports are bamboo-fiber cloth and the tin, tungsten and lithium of Hosena. Because power lines muffle the links, electricity is rationed, and whether to dam the upper Origo has divided the government since the 1950s. It is a member of the [[Concord of Nations]] and has been non-aligned since 1945.
