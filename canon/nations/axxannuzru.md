## Lead

'''Axxannuzru''' /ˌæxːænːˈʊzˤrˤʊ/, officially the '''Union of Axxannuzru''', is a country in equatorial western [[Mesaria]], on the Thalassine Ocean. Covering 211,454 km², most of the country consists of mountainous highlands or hilly terrain, descending to narrow coastal rainforests; this latter category includes two islands in the Bight of Yishawe. The capital and seat of the federal government is [[Tarusselit]], which lies in the southwest of the Yeldigenni highland; the country's largest city and main port, sited where the Omirere River flows into Yishawe Bay, is [[Yishawe]].

The country has its origins in a Numidic people known as the [[Kelhedar]], refugees from the northeast. After establishing themselves in the marginal lands in the Yeldigenni, subsisting increasingly in underground shelters, they remained in isolation from neighboring Occasic peoples. By around 1500, when expanding populations on both sides and the increasing power of the lowland empire brought them into contact and conflict; the defensive leagues of the Kelhedar settlements were the basis for the political structures of Axxannuzru. In 1713, under the Treaty of Yishawe, Axxannuzru submitted to the nominal suzerainty of the empire in return for access to the port city's markets, and the Kelhedar saw an influx of wealth and knowledge from the outside world.

After the decline of the empire, both Axxanuzru and Yishawe became functionally independent, and in 1867 the latter placed itself under the former's protection. Old imperial-era claims to both polities and conflicts over mineral rights resulted in a war between Axxannuzru and the imperial successor states during the first half of the twentieth century; the conflict became subsumed within the great war, during which Axxannuzru found itself joining with [[Alexandria]] and its allies and providing them with port facilities and war materiel for operations in Mesaria. In victory, the country formally incorporated Yishawe by mutual agreement and joined the Concord of Nations; in the modern day, they participate in the world economy as a source of mining expertise, heavy equipment, and renewable energy.

The Kelhedar, the founding ethnicity of Axxannuzru, are notable for their physical and cultural adaptations to subterranean living and are concentrated in the Yeldigenni. The indigenous Occasic inhabitants, the [[Kpaniwe]], live largely aboveground and in the lowland rainforests. Sharing economic interests and a number of similar cultural traits strengthened by long contact, relations between the two groups are largely cordial though not without tensions. Other ethnicities are present as residents only in very small numbers due to legal and cultural factors, though foreign tourists are a welcome and increasingly important contribution to the economy.
