'''AI use on Iceria''' sets out what we ask of anyone who uses AI tools on the wiki. It sits under the [[Charter]], 5.4.

Stewards use AI tools to help draft some pages and make some maps.<ref name="charter" /> The [[Word from the Founder]] tells that story.

== The rules ==
# Check every fact. AI gets names, dates and rules wrong. Check them against the wiki before you publish.
# You own what you post. "The AI got it wrong" is not a defense.
# No bulk dumps. Add pages you have read and can answer for. Talk to the community before a large addition.
# Your character's choices are yours. AI can help you draft them, but the decisions should be your own.

== What works ==
* Drafting a page from your own notes and ideas, or from work you develop with other members
* Checking a page against the [[Geography of Iceria]], [[History of Iceria]] and [[World Standards]] pages
* Editing and tidying text
* Brainstorming names and ideas

== What does not ==
* Writing about Iceria lore you have not read
* Posting without checking the facts
* Generic text with no link to Iceria's own places and people
* Letting AI decide a political outcome for your nation

== Labels ==
No page carries a label for AI use. Nobody may be attacked for using AI, and nobody may be pressured to use it.<ref name="charter" />

== Questions ==
Ask on this page's talk page, or on the [https://discord.gg/T6MdbSJXue Discord server]. If you want to know whether a page used AI, ask. You will get an honest answer.
