The '''culture zones of Iceria''' are the 31 regions of the [[Geography of Iceria|world]] map that
show who lives where. Each zone is home to one culture family, a group of related peoples with an
in-world name and real Earth cultures as its inspiration, called its anchors. The Hesperic peoples
of Eostria's oceanic west coast draw on France, Britain, the Low Countries and Germany; the
Erythraean traders of Mesaria's east coast draw on the Swahili, Somali and Malagasy; the Seric
peoples of the east draw on Korea, Japan and Manchuria. When a player founds a nation, its zone suggests its people, languages and names, and a
nation that differs explains why.<ref name="ws-zones" />

Sixteen zones lie in the Old World of Eostria, Mesaria and Solandra, where humankind began and the
first cities arose. The other fifteen lie on the frontier continents of Septentria, Meridia and
Selenara, where native peoples met settlers from the Old World after about 1450, so each of those
zones names both.<ref name="history" />

== How the zones work ==
The zones cover the whole of Iceria's land outside the Polar Treaty Zone, 165.4 million km². The
culture-zone map is a strong default: a nation usually shares the culture of its zone, and one
that differs, such as Thulean settlers on a tropical coast, explains why. Cultures blend where
zones meet, and mixed cultures are common there; the official map settles which zone a place lies
in. The rules for nations are set out in the [[World Standards]].<ref name="ws-zones" />

The anchors name where a family's inspiration comes from, not what its people must be. Each
family's languages, institutions and history are its own, and Iceria's peoples go by the in-world
family names; the Earth names in the tables below are anchors only. [[Names of Iceria]] lists all 31 families on one page.

== The Old World ==
Humankind began in the Old World, in the high country around Mesaria's Dorsal Range. Farming and
the first cities began about five thousand years before 2000 in four river lands: the Origo
valley in Mesaria, the lower Aurorea in Eostria, the twin rivers of Solandra and the warm Seric
south.<ref name="history" />

=== Eostria ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-eostria.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Eostria]]
Eostria's seven zones run from the oceanic west coast on the Thalassine Ocean through the
continental heartland to the cold far north and the east. The west and north-west coasts on the
Thalassine are Hesperic and Thulean country; the wide continental plains belong to the Sarmatic
family, and the tundra and taiga beyond 62° N to the Cimmerian. The Ausonic family lives on two
warm coasts, the south-west corner on the Thalassine and the south coast on the Ecliptic, which
has the Old World's only large Mediterranean climate. South of the plains lies the dry Scythic
interior, and beyond 120° E the Seric east.<ref name="czmap" />

=== Mesaria ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-mesaria.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Mesaria]]
Mesaria's six zones follow its belts of climate. The dry north along the Origo is Numidic, and
the savanna and forest of the west, toward the Thalassine, are Occasic. The Silvanic family lives
in the rainforest core and the Dorsalic in the cool highlands of the Dorsal Range. On the east
peninsula, the trade coast of the Ecliptic Ocean, live the Erythraean peoples, and on the dry
southern cape the Notian.<ref name="czmap" />

=== Solandra ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-solandra.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Solandra]]
Solandra has three zones. The drier north and west, where the continent's first cities grew
between its twin rivers, is Solandric, and the rainforest of the south and east is Chrysic. The Nesiotic family lives on the coasts and islands; its sailors crossed to
Selenara's north coast in the first millennium and settled there.<ref name="czmap" /><ref name="history" />

== The frontier continents ==
Septentria, Meridia and Selenara were settled by their native peoples many thousands of years
before the Old World's ships arrived, and great native civilizations rose in the highlands of the
Altissima and of Meridia. From about 1450 the sea powers of the Old World reached them. Contact
between 1450 and 1550 was followed by conquest, and diseases from the Old World killed far more
native people than war did. Colonies spread through the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, and
a slave trade carried large numbers of Mesarian people across the Thalassine to the plantation
coasts. Most of Septentria and Meridia won independence between about 1780 and 1830.<ref name="history" />

Each frontier zone therefore names both its native peoples and the settlers who came, and records
which way it leans in 2000: '''mostly native''', where native peoples are the large majority and
hold most of the land; '''mixed''', where both are large, native nations hold big homelands or most
people are of mixed descent; or '''mostly settler''', where settler descendants are the majority
and native nations hold treaty lands or homelands. This is a default, not a rule: a nation there
may be native, settler or mixed, and a player's story may differ, explained in a line.<ref name="ws-zones" />
By area, three zones covering 14.3 million km² lean mostly native, eight covering 45.3 million km²
are mixed, and four covering 30.7 million km² lean mostly settler.

=== Septentria ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-septentria.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Septentria]]
Ships from Eostria's west coast crossed the Thalassine to Septentria. The Nemorine zone in the
east, with the finest natural harbor in the world at the mouth of the
Aeterna<ref name="atlas" /> and wide farmland, drew the largest settler wave. The highland Altissiman peoples were conquered but
not replaced: most people there descend from them, and native languages are spoken beside Ausonic
ones, so the zone counts as mixed. The Nivalic north, with nothing to farm, stayed native. The Corallian plantation coasts are home to many descendants of Mesarian peoples brought
by the slave trade.<ref name="czmap" /><ref name="history" />

=== Meridia ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-meridia.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Meridia]]
Occasic sailors reached Meridia from Mesaria's south-west cape in the fourteenth century, and the
later fleets followed their route. Settlers held the coasts of the Fluvian rainforest while native
nations kept its deep interior. The Orean highland peoples, many and conquered but not replaced,
remain the majority. The dry Siccan zone drew few settlers, and Ecliptic trading towns line its
east coast. The temperate Mitian south-east drew both settler waves, and Thulean settlers took the
sheltered valleys of the Tempestan south-west.<ref name="czmap" /><ref name="history" />

=== Selenara ===
[[File:Iceria-culture-selenara.png|thumb|upright=1.6|The culture zones of Selenara]]
Selenara's first peoples came across the sea in very early times, and in the first millennium
Nesiotic sailors settled its north coast. Solandran and Nesiotic settlers were there before the
Thalassine powers, whose ships came by
the westerlies of the far south. Settler cities grew on the Eremic Mediterranean coast and settler towns
on the warmer Gelidan coasts, and the Pelagian east runs on a treaty partnership beside independent island
nations. No large Old World settler wave reached the Selenic north coast, where
Austronesian-anchored peoples, long joined by Nesiotic newcomers and Ecliptic traders, are the
majority.<ref name="czmap" /><ref name="history" />
