## Lead

'''The first five nations to confirm an official welcome for an envoy''' will be offered a personally commissioned friendship presentation in honor of Tleyew-Pas's five civic centers: a letter of friendship, a paired Ironfleece-and-Brightwool woven set, a copper keepsake and a small ceremonial pennant designed to celebrate both nations. They will also receive an invitation to explore a reciprocal cultural, educational or maritime exchange. Each presentation will be shaped with the host, not imposed upon it.
</div>

An envoy's visit brings no treaty, alliance or trade obligation. Any larger gift, including heritage animals, requires separate consent, careful preparation and appropriate veterinary passage; neither the protected Pasyel nor the sacred Pavar is ever offered.</div>
