## Lead

'''Tleyew-Pas''' (pronounced ''tlay-yew-paw'') is a democratic, matriarchal parliamentary republic in '''southern Meridia'''. The country comprises one coastal and inland-upland mainland territory and three nearby offshore islands, with an area of approximately '''2,592,778 km²''' and an estimated population of '''850,000 in 2000'''. Its lands lie predominantly within the Tempestan cultural zone, with Siccan, Mitian and Orean zones also represented.

From its highland passes and working valleys to sheltered coastal communities, Tleyew-Pas is shaped by generations of shepherds, weavers, horse breeders, navigators and animal-health practitioners. Its characteristic heritage breeds are the naturally black-fleeced '''Tleyew-Pas Ironfleece''', the fine pale-fleeced '''Tleyew-Pas Brightwool''', the sacred brown '''Pavar''' mountain horse and the taupe-silver '''Racer'''. The protected wild '''Pasyel''' raptor appears in the national flag and represents freedom, vigilance and respect for life beyond human use. Along the coast, the protected '''Azharuun''' shark and '''Ilyavai''' dolphin are recognized as free-ranging members of the same living watershed. In the foundational legend of the departing '''Tleyewpas''', three living kin inherit its guardianship: the '''Pasyel''' of the sky, the '''Pavar''' of the land and the '''Azharuun''' of the waters. This is a spiritual relationship within national folklore, not a claim of biological descent or human ownership of the animals.

The republic is governed by an elected Parliament, a constitutional President and a Prime Minister responsible to the legislature. Its secular '''Charter of the Living Bond''' connects civil equality and political accountability with obligations toward water, land, domestic animals, wildlife and future generations. Alongside its documented civic history, Tleyew-Pas preserves an extensive body of oral traditions concerning the '''Great Rootway''', the '''Spore Spirits''' and the legendary '''Tleyewpas'''. Among these are two contrasting accounts of human-animal bonds: the openly shared '''First Calling''' of the Pavar and the guarded coastal '''Answer Beneath''', associated with the Azharuun and the mysterious '''Withwater Wardens'''.

Across the land, staffed highland watchtowers and mounted guardians protect the roads and communities. From Vaelthara, a technologically sophisticated navy and merchant fleet carry the republic's protection, goods and invitations far beyond its shores. Tleyew-Pas is geographically distant, but it is not isolated.
