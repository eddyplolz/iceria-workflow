## Lead

'''Alexandria''', officially the '''Empire of the Alexandrians''', is a country in the south-west corner of Eostria, on the Thalassine Ocean. It covers 1,309,525 km² on the mainland and the [[Les Îles de la Couronne|Crown Isles]] off its coasts, and had about 80.5 million people in 2000. Its capital and largest city is Geneva, the ancient Leria, which stands where the Corcovado meets the Lère; its great harbor, Port-Réal, lies at the river's mouth. To the north it borders [[Rossheim-Amannia|Rossheim]]. Alexandria is a plurinational state of two equal peoples, the Alexandrians and the Martino, and is also home to the Mendiar of the south-eastern mountains.

The country descends from the Lerian Republic, founded in the lower valley of the Lère around 480 BC. The Republic became an empire that at its greatest extent ruled the Ausonic and Hesperic lands and most of the Numidic lands across the sea, and gave the [[Culture zones of Iceria|Ausonic]] lands their classical language, but its heartland was never conquered, and its old families became the valley's kings. In 680 the marriage of Isolde of Baudrix and Francis Joseph of Valenciennes founded the kingdom. One crown has ruled from Geneva since 1212, and the Compact of Los Santos of 1534 joined the Martino hill country on its own terms. From the 1480s Alexandria was one of the great colonial powers, and in 1715 its rulers took the title of emperor. A revolution overthrew the throne in 1774, but in 1782 the exiled heir crowned himself Emperor Francis Joseph III and kept the revolution's gains in a civil code.

In the nineteenth century Alexandria built a second empire along the coasts of Mesaria. It fought among the victors of the [[Great War]] and was a leading founder of the [[Concord of Nations]] in 1945. Its colonies were independent by 1970, and it heads the Communauté alexandrine, a league of the Alexandrian-speaking states. Under the Constitution of 1978 Alexandria is a parliamentary monarchy: Emperor [[Edgard II]] is head of state, and the First Consul leads the government and answers to the Imperial Cortes. Its ten regions govern themselves through elected regional Cortes; the two Martino regions share one, the Cortes of the Hills.

Alexandria has a high-income economy, the busiest port on the Thalassine and one of the world's great mineral districts, and it draws most of its electricity from nuclear power. It is among the most visited countries in the world. Geneva is also the see of the Pontiff of Leria, head of the Lerian Soterian Church, to which about 72% of Alexandrians belong.
