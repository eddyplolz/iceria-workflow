## Lead

'''Tuvallia''', officially the '''Tuvallian National State''', is a country in south-western [[Eostria]], on a cold mountain peninsula.<ref name="zones" /><ref name="map" /> It borders [[Alexandria]] to the west and [[Rossheim-Amannia]] to the north and includes two large islands and their islets off its southern coast, about 609,000 km² in all, and had about 28 million people in 2000. The Hrbet, a high ice-capped massif, fills the interior of the peninsula, and most Tuvallians live in the lowlands and valleys around it. The capital, Hohenwacht, is a walled fortress city on a spur above the gorge of the Mirava, built where the road from the sea climbs into the mountains; the port and second city, Mirmünde, lies at the mouth of its own small river on the [[Thalassine Ocean]]. The country is divided into seven provinces under governors named by the ruling party, and a military border district along the Alexandrian frontier is governed by the army.

The first people of the peninsula were hill peoples who spoke the language now called Horan, and their descendants still live in the high valleys of the interior. In antiquity the peninsula was a province of the Lerian Empire,<ref name="alexleria" /> and the Soterian faith reached it under Lerian rule. When the empire gave up its provinces, warrior orders came over the mountains into the ruins of Lerian rule, conquered the valleys and made the hill peoples their subjects, and around 1300 their leaders founded the Kingdom of Tuvallia. In the sixteenth century its kings broke with the Pontiff of Leria<ref name="alexrel" /> and made themselves heads of a national church. For most of its history Tuvallia was ruled by warrior kings and their general staff and lived on a war footing. Border warfare with Alexandria over the high country between them continued for centuries and ended with the War of the Passes of 1866 to 1868, an Alexandrian victory that fixed the border where it runs today.

In the [[Great War]] of 1938 to 1945 Tuvallia and the [[Jagoton Compact]] fought together as one bloc, and the bloc lost.<ref name="greatwar" /> The army held a static mountain front on the Alexandrian border for most of the war, and its fleet and bombers failed in a campaign to close the Alexandrian harbor of Port-Réal.<ref name="alexwar" /> Tuvallia signed an armistice in 1945 before any enemy army had crossed its borders, and many Tuvallians came to believe that the war had been lost at home rather than in the field. The peace took its fleet, capped its army, imposed reparations and placed its eastern provinces, about 150,000 to 200,000 km² on the coast to the north-east, under a trusteeship of the [[Concord of Nations]], which Tuvallia still disputes. In 1945 and 1946 the army forced the king to abdicate, ended the monarchy and built the party state that still rules. When the winners split over the colonies and the minerals,<ref name="divided" /> Tuvallia joined neither half: it sold tungsten to both and sought softer terms from each, but gained little. Outside the Concord while it refused inspection, it was admitted in 1964 once the cap on its army had been verified, and it has contested the trusteeship at every session since.

The National Front of Tuvallia is the only legal party. It governs in the name of national unity and of the wrongs done to Tuvallia in 1945, but real power rests with the army, whose factions removed the party's leader in 1962 and again in 1983. The present chairman, Gerhard Steinbrecher, a former commander of the mountain troops who came to power in the coup of 1983, is in his seventies. He rarely appears in public and has named no successor. The standing army stays under the treaty cap, but universal service has built very large reserves, and the mountain troops are its elite. The state owns the tin and tungsten mines, the steel and arms works and the dams that supply most of the country's electricity, and the hill country raises sheep for wool. Tuvallian is the language of the state, the army and the towns; Horan, the tongue of the hill people, the Horani, is tolerated but is not taught in schools. Most Tuvallians belong to the Tuvallian Soterian Church.
