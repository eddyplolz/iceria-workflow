## 3.8 Your nation only
'''(a) Your own nation.''' Players control their own nations and characters, and nothing else. No "godmodding": you may not decide what other nations do, say, or suffer, or claim support nobody gave you.

'''(b) Shared events.''' Events that touch several nations need the agreement of each of them.

'''(c) Contradictions.''' If one nation's story contradicts another's pages, a steward settles it, and each nation's own pages decide the facts about that nation, apart from what an agreed treaty or event settles (1.4).

'''(d) Revising your history.''' You may revise your own nation's history at any time, except where another nation's published page relies on it; then agree the change with that nation first.

## 5.4 AI assistance
AI-assisted work is welcome. Stewards use AI tools to help draft some pages and make some maps; the [[Word from the Founder]] says so and names the tools. Nobody may be attacked for using AI, and nobody may be pressured to use it. [[AI use on Iceria]] sets out what we ask of anyone who uses AI tools.

## 5.6 Nation pages
A nation's pages belong to its creator. Others may fix typos, formatting, and links; anything more goes on the talk page first. Any member may add or remove their own line in the Participants section of a nation's article, and the creator or a steward may remove the line of a member who is not taking part ([[Manual of Style]] 8.5). Shared pages are open to everyone. A released nation's pages may also be edited as 4.4 allows.
