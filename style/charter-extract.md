# Charter (extract)

Copied from the Iceria wiki page "Charter" (revision 3258) on 2026-11-01. The live page outranks this copy. Only the clauses a member drafting pages needs are here; the full Charter is on the wiki.

## 1.4 What counts as canon
What counts as canon, in order of authority:
# the official maps ([[World Standards]] 7.4) and the canonical geography;
# this charter and the decision log;
# treaties and events that every party involved has agreed to, for what they settle;
# each nation's accepted proposal, its entry in the [[Local canon register]], and its wiki pages and wiki news stories, for other facts about that nation.

Canon lives on the wiki. In-character posts on Discord are where nations play, and they count as activity (4.1); what happens there becomes canon once it is written on the wiki. Other Discord conversation is table talk. A missing page means "not written yet," never "doesn't exist."

## 3.1 Joining
Anyone 18 or older may found a nation. They:
# join the Iceria Discord server, where nations are discussed;
# paint their claim in the '''[https://studio.iceria.org Claim Studio]''', which runs in the browser or as a download and checks the claim as it is painted, until its pre-check passes; and
# post a short proposal from the proposal template, as the [[Nation proposals]] page describes: the nation's name, its claim, its culture (or its twist on the culture of its zone), a short history, its capital, and an answer to at least one of three questions about the nation.

A person may have one open proposal at a time. The stewards decline a proposal that is generic, copied, or leaves its questions unanswered, whatever tools were used to write it, and a declined proposal may be fixed and posted again at any time.

## 3.8 Your nation only
'''(a) Your own nation.''' Players control their own nations and characters, and nothing else. No "godmodding": you may not decide what other nations do, say, or suffer, or claim support nobody gave you.

'''(b) Shared events.''' Events that touch several nations need the agreement of each of them.

'''(c) Contradictions.''' If one nation's story contradicts another's pages, a steward settles it, and each nation's own pages decide the facts about that nation, apart from what an agreed treaty or event settles (1.4).

'''(d) Revising your history.''' You may revise your own nation's history at any time, except where another nation's published page relies on it; then agree the change with that nation first.

## 5.4 AI assistance
AI-assisted work is welcome. Stewards use AI tools to help draft some pages and make some maps; the [[Word from the Founder]] says so and names the tools. Nobody may be attacked for using AI, and nobody may be pressured to use it. [[AI use on Iceria]] sets out what we ask of anyone who uses AI tools.

## 5.6 Nation pages
A nation's pages belong to its creator. Others may fix typos, formatting, and links; anything more goes on the talk page first. Any member may add or remove their own line in the Participants section of a nation's article, and the creator or a steward may remove the line of a member who is not taking part ([[Manual of Style]] 8.5). Shared pages are open to everyone. A released nation's pages may also be edited as 4.4 allows.

## 6.1 The rules
# Treat others as you'd want to be treated. This includes passive-aggression and "joking" digs.
# Never reveal anyone's real name, photo or other personal details unless they have made them public.
# Keep in-character and out-of-character apart. Nations may insult each other; people may not.
# Bring problems with the rules up early, instead of springing a loophole later.
# Don't import drama from other servers or communities.
# Critique the work, never the tools used to make it.
# Keep everything safe for work.
# No spam, no mass pings, and no advertising without a steward's OK.
# One account per person. A second account needs a steward's OK, and votes and claims made through an undisclosed one are void.
# Use a readable name, so others can tag you.
# On the Discord server, follow Discord's Community Guidelines.
