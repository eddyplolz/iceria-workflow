# Interview the member before a draft

1. **Read first.** Read the nation's page in `canon/nations/`, the matching template in
   `templates/`, and the `canon/` pages the subject touches (geography, culture zone, shared
   history, the calendar). Done when you know which facts canon already fixes, so you never ask
   the member for one of them.
2. **Ask one question at a time**, in the template's section order, and wait for the answer. Ask
   about what the page needs and canon leaves open: names, dates, figures, who leads, how it began.
3. **When the member is undecided, offer 2 or 3 options** that fit canon, each in one line, and
   let them pick or write their own. A political outcome or a character's choice is always theirs.
4. **Flag a clash the moment it appears.** When an answer conflicts with `canon/`, quote the page
   and ask which they mean. When an answer touches another nation (a border, a treaty, a war, a
   shared event), say it needs that nation's agreement before it is canon (Charter 3.8, 5.6), and
   offer a version that keeps to the member's own nation.
5. **Confirm a summary.** List every fact the member gave, each in one line, marked NEW or with the
   `canon/` page it matches, and the ones that need another nation's agreement. Ask the member to
   correct or confirm it.

Done when the member confirms the summary. Its NEW facts start the NEW-facts list for
`iceria-draft`, which drafts from the summary.
