## Skills

Six skills in `.claude/skills/`. The first four carry the review loop's first two steps:

- `iceria-interview`: asking the member about a page, one question at a time, before a draft.
- `iceria-draft`: drafting a page for the member's nation from their notes.
- `iceria-canon-check`: matching every claim in a draft to a page in `canon/`.
- `iceria-self-audit`: the style and AI-tell pass before the member reads the draft.
- `iceria-advisor`: answering "can I do this?" with a citation from the Charter or `canon/`.
- `iceria-report`: writing a problem report the member sends when the kit gets something wrong.
